About

Connecting materials, structures and sustainability

SMS is a research group within the Faculty of Civil Engineering at The University of Danang – University of Science and Technology, Vietnam. Our research addresses practical challenges in civil engineering.

Our mission

To advance research and knowledge transfer in construction materials, structural engineering and sustainable development, while educating the next generation of civil engineers and researchers.

We combine experiments, analytical methods, numerical modelling, machine learning and interdisciplinary approaches to address real-world infrastructure challenges.

Research interests

Our research themes

Research spanning sustainable materials, structural performance and data-driven engineering.

🌿

Green, low-carbon construction materials

Developing environmentally responsible materials to reduce emissions and improve sustainability in construction.

🧪

High-performance and smart construction materials

Exploring advanced materials with enhanced performance, specialised functions and adaptive capabilities for modern structures.

🌊

Durability and service life of reinforced concrete structures

Investigating structural durability in aggressive environments, particularly marine exposure, to improve reliability and service life.

🧱

Structural strengthening with sustainable and novel materials

Developing strengthening solutions for existing structures using FRP, FRCM/TRM and other advanced material systems.

🏗️

Plate, shell and tensegrity structures

Investigating the mechanics, modelling, analysis and applications of lightweight, efficient structural systems.

🏢

Earthquake engineering and seismic resilience

Developing and evaluating solutions to improve earthquake resistance, structural performance and safety.

🤖

AI and machine learning for structures and sustainable materials

Applying data-driven methods, machine learning and AI to prediction, optimisation, reliability assessment and the development of civil engineering solutions.
